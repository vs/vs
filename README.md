# Semyon Vadishev

Software Engineer · Former CTO · Early-stage startups · Backend, IoT, developer tools

I build products from the first line of code through launch, sales and support.

I am a software engineer with 17+ years of experience. For nine of them I was CTO of TMate Software, a bootstrapped and profitable six-person company that makes developer tools for Subversion and Git.

At TMate I started SubGit, a tool that moves large development teams from Subversion to Git. Over 100,000 teams have used it, including teams at SpaceX, Boeing, Samsung and AT&T. I implemented the core of the synchronization, joined sales calls as the technical expert and was the first line of support for high-profile customers.

Since 2024 I have worked with Divital, a senior-care startup in Germany. I wrote the whole backend of its sensor and alarm platform. The prototype helped secure the 2025 seed round and went into a pilot at a care facility for people with dementia.

## Open-source projects
