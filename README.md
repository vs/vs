# Semyon Vadishev

Software Engineer · Ex-CTO · Building products with AI agents · Backend, IoT, developer tools

I am a software engineer with 17+ years of experience. For nine of them I was CTO of TMate Software, a bootstrapped and profitable six-person company that makes developer tools for Subversion and Git.

At TMate I started SubGit, a tool that moves large companies from Subversion to Git. I wrote the core of its synchronization. When enterprise customers started buying it, I joined their sales calls as the technical expert and was the first line of support for the largest of them. Over 100,000 teams have used SubGit, including teams at SpaceX, Boeing, Samsung and AT&T.

In 2024 I joined Divital, a senior-care startup in Germany, and wrote the whole backend of its sensor and alarm platform. The prototype helped secure the 2025 seed round and went into a pilot at a care facility for people with dementia. In December 2025 I stepped back from hands-on development, but kept in touch with the team to consult on the platform and its deployment.

## Open-source projects

- **[Assup](https://github.com/vs/assup)** - self-hosted portfolio manager for Interactive Brokers that I use daily to manage my investments, evaluate risk and prepare tax reports. Built with TypeScript, Node.js, React and PostgreSQL.
- **[Picode](https://github.com/vs/picode)** - hides a short ID inside a photo, invisible to people but readable by a phone camera, as an alternative to QR codes. Built with Python and PyTorch, with an iOS scanner that decodes on the device.
- **[Telegrowl](https://github.com/vs/telegrowl)** - hands-free Telegram voice client for iOS, for talking to AI assistants while driving. Built with Swift and SwiftUI.
